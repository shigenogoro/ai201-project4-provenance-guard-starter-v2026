First of all, people like to have a good quality relationship, and spending more time communicating via technology can destroy their relationship. For example, when I hung out with my friends, we always create a lot of happy memories among us. Furthermore, when my friend is in his downtime, meeting face to face, or even a hug can always encourage them to overcome their challenges. All of these experiences can lead to a better quality of the relationship. On the contrary, communication via text message cannot let us sense others feeling. Therefore, even though my friend told me they are fine to not make me worry about them, I won’t be able to discern it. Then we will miss the chance to strengthen our friendship. Technology can undermine people’s relationships.
