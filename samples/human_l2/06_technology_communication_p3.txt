Last but not least, people care about having their own time, and spending more time communicating with technology will reduce the time that they can stay alone. Take me for example, I need a lot of alone time for me to reflect and study. When my friends kept chatting with me via Messenger, the notification always drives me crazy. As a result, I can’t relax. On the other hand, if I reduce the time communicating with technology, I will be able to enjoy the peace in my room. Then we can have better communication next time when I met my friends. We should spend less time communicating with technology.
