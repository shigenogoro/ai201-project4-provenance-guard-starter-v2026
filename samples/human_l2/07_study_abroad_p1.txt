First of all, universities and colleges care about students’ future, and encouraging them to spend at least one semester studying in a foreign country can achieve this goal. For example, most of the students in Taiwan need to speak fluent English after they graduate from university, because most of the companies in Taiwan need to do business with foreign countries. If students spend at least one semester in a foreign country, such as United State, they will need to speak English in their daily life. To be more specific, they can practice their English skills when they want to buy food. Furthermore, they will be required to have English discussions in the class. Therefore, they will be able to improve their English skills because of many opportunities for them to practice every day.
