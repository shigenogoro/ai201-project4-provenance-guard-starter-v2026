First of all, it is important to consider people’s safety when they go to school, and having clothing rules can make sure people’s safety. For example, when I was an elementary school student, I needed to wear a uniform to school. With the uniform, teachers and the parents who were volunteers can help me cross the road safely. Moreover, when I met a creepy guy who follow me, the staff in the convenience store helped me to contact the school and kept me from harassment. Therefore, I can go to school safely every day. On the other hand, if I didn’t wear a uniform, those people who spotted that creepy guy won’t be able to help me out directly. As a result, it would be very dangerous for me.
